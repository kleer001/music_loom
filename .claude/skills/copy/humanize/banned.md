# Banned constructions — music_loom

Overlay on the house list. Entries here add bans; entries under `## Exceptions`
strike words the house list would otherwise flag.

## Exceptions

- harness — names a specific piece of apparatus in this repo: `rack/R3-measure`,
  the render-and-measure harness an instrument copies in. It is a concrete noun
  here, not the promotional verb ("harness the power of") the house ban targets,
  and it is the word the rack's own README uses.
