### Important Notes

1. **Remake FLPs vs. Original Samples:** Many free FLPs are remakes or tutorials that deconstruct official songs (e.g., Skrillex remakes). These are legal to study and learn from; redistributing the original copyrighted samples is not.

2. **Dependencies:** Many FLPs require specific plugins:
   - Serum (Xfer Records)
   - Massive/Massive X (Native Instruments)
   - Sylenth1 (LennarDigital)
   - Spire (Reveal Sound)
   - Check FLP description for required plugins before download

3. **Commercial Use:** Most free FLPs are marked for personal/educational use only. Verify the license before using in commercial projects.

4. **FL Studio Version:** Check FLP version requirements (e.g., FL 20+); older projects may not open in newer versions without conversion.

5. **Sample Packs:** Many projects reference specific sample packs. The FLP itself is free, but required samples may be sold separately—check dependencies.
