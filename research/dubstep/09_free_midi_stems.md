## Quick Start Workflow

### For MIDI Composition
1. Start with **W.A. Production - FREE MIDI Mega Pack** (270 MIDI loops)
2. Layer with **Ghosthack - Free Dubstep Sounds 2025** (140 BPM patterns)
3. Add basslines from **Unison Audio** or **Subaqueous Music** drum MIDI

### For Remix/Remix Learning
1. Source stems from **SongStems.net** (artist-organized dubstep tracks)
2. Explore **Beatport contest archives** for official stems
3. Backup archive at **Internet Archive** for rare/hard-to-find riddim stems

### For Production Templates
1. Download **Free Sample Packs - Melodic Dubstep** (includes Ableton template + kits)
2. Layer with **Ghosthack Ultimate Freebie** (1000+ additional sounds)
3. Supplement with **Function Loops** construction kits for variation

### For Learning/Education
1. **Cambridge MT Mixing Secrets Library** (minimal-processing multitracks)
2. **ccMixter stems** (CC-licensed, proper workflow study)
3. **W.A. Production packs** (production-ready examples)
