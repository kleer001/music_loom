## Summary by Use Case

### Starting a Dubstep Kit from Scratch
1. **Drums:** Ghosthack (200+ free) or Cymatics Dubstep Starter Pack (200+ samples)
2. **Bass:** Ghosthack Dubstep Bass Hits (30 one-shots) + Wobbles from Looperman
3. **FX:** Ghosthack Risers & Impacts + Pixabay CC0 foley
4. **Synth:** Download free Vital + load 75 factory presets or 21k PresetShare presets
5. **Vocals:** Free Vocals acapellas or Looperman CC-licensed chops

### Best for CC0 / Public Domain
- Freesound.org (filter by CC0)
- Pixabay Audio (all MP3s are CC0)
- Wikimedia Commons (filter by CC0)
- ZapSplat (subset of CC0; majority standard license)

### Best Value (Most Samples, Free)
- Ghosthack (500+ free dubstep samples across many packs)
- Cymatics (100+ free drum kits)
- PresetShare (21,000+ free Vital presets)
- SampleSwap (9.4 GB of WAV samples)

### Fastest Download
- Pixabay (MP3, CC0, no registration)
- Ghosthack (direct WAV download)
- 99Sounds (direct WAV, pay-what-you-want)

## License Reference

| License | Attribution | Commercial Use | Remixing |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|----------|
| **CC0** | Not required | Yes | Yes |
| **CC-BY** | Required | Yes | Yes |
| **CC-BY-SA** | Required | Yes | Yes (output must be SA) |
| **Royalty-free** | Usually not | Yes | Often yes |
| **Public Domain** | Not required | Yes | Yes |

**Important:** When using samples from Freesound or SampleSwap, **verify the individual sound's license**—not all are CC0. Pixabay Audio and Wikimedia Commons audio are generally CC0, but check per-item. Ghosthack, 99Sounds, Cymatics, and Looperman are all royalty-free (no per-sound licensing ambiguity).
