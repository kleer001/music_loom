# Hindustani raga

## 1. Tuning

Not primarily a tuning problem, but not 12-TET either: the microtonal inflection of individual notes is part of a raga's identity rather than expressive deviation from a grid. A fixed-pitch rendering is audibly a different thing.

## 2. Instrumentation

The **tanpura** drone is a designed spectral effect. Its bridge, the **javari**, is curved so a plucked string makes periodic grazing contact with the surface, and a cotton thread (jiva) under each string sets the node precisely ([Jivari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jivari); [Darbar, *The Tanpura: One Note Wonder*](https://darbar.org/the-tanpura-one-note-wonder/)).

The mechanism has a property a static waveshaper does not: **the grazing point shifts up the sloping bridge as the string's amplitude decays**, so overtone content evolves over the life of the note as a function of amplitude. High-speed filming at 10 000 fps (IIT) reports the jiva "not only brings out a richer set of overtones, but is clearly marked by a definite change in the pattern of how overtones evolve over time" (reported via Darbar). Sitar and surbahar use the same treatment on their drone strings.

A spectrally alive drone is doing structural work: it is the reference the melodic pitches are heard against.

## 3. Performance structure

A raga carries more than a pitch collection ([Arohana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arohana) and the raga entries it links):

- **Aroha / avaroha** — ascending and descending forms, frequently *asymmetrical*: a note present going up may be absent or approached differently coming down.
- **Vadi / samvadi** — principal and secondary notes carrying melodic gravity. Not a tonic in the functional-harmony sense; notes the line returns to and rests on.
- **Pakad** — signature phrases that identify the raga. Recognition rests on these more than on the note set.
- **Gamaka** — oscillations, glides and grace inflections, described consistently as constitutive rather than decorative.

The form (alap → development → faster sections) unfolds over a long span from sparse and unmetered toward dense and metered.

Representationally that is an ascent/descent asymmetry, a weighting over notes, a phrase vocabulary, and a specification of motion *between* notes. An array of intervals keeps roughly the first.
