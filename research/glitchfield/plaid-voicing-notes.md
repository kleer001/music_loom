# Plaid — synth voicing research notes

Reference for GLITCHFIELD voicing work. Compiled from a five-agent web research pass over
Plaid's discography (plus their Black Dog roots), focused on **melodic/harmonic voicing**.
**No code changes are implied by this file.**

Plaid are the **melodic** center of glitch/IDM — the sound GLITCHFIELD is aiming at — so this
is the most directly applicable of the artist studies.

Confidence tags: **HIGH** = band-stated or strong technical source · **MED** = credible
secondary / reconstruction · **LOW** = fan/aesthetic inference. Note: Plaid discuss *process*
(gear, composition philosophy) openly but almost never music-theory specifics, so chord-level
detail is mostly MED/LOW inference, honestly flagged.

Recurring primary sources: Inverted Audio interview ("where they find their melodies"),
Denver Westword (Andy Turner), Tape Op #24, Warp "What Makes Plaid," MusicRadar studio feature,
Equipboard, plus Hooktheory/Tunebat for a few documented keys.

---

## The headline: this is the one that matches "melodic glitch"

Unlike Autechre (generative, scale-forced, anti-12-TET) or Aphex (microtuning), Plaid are
**human songwriters first**:

- **Melody/harmony first, sound design second.** They compose on **piano**, then build the
  patch. "Most stuff is played, not melodic loops." [HIGH]
- Self-described **"explorers, not writers"** — untrained, learn by ear, "slowly trying to find
  the note and the chord that comes after the previous chord." Now use **Scaler 2** to explore
  chords. [HIGH]
- **Polyphony is an explicit value** (Polymer's manifesto: "Polyphony, Pollution, Politics"). [MED]
- **Consonant, diatonic/modal, bittersweet.** Major-leaning melody over minor/modal context =
  modal mixture. Triadic core, added 6ths/sus for warmth; **not** dense jazz extensions (that's
  more Squarepusher). [MED]
- **12-TET, no microtuning** documented — the opposite of Autechre/Aphex. [MED, from silence]

**So for GLITCHFIELD's melodic-glitch goal, actual chords ARE appropriate** — the chord-voicing
revamp is far more faithful to Plaid than it was to Autechre.

## Per-release voicing notes (highlights)

- **Bytes / Spanners (Black Dog):** DX7 FM + acid filter sweeps; "mastery of abstract melodies"
  over loop/sample harmony; sultry pads vs crisp acid drums. Mostly textural, not classical
  voicing. [gear HIGH / voicing MED]
- **Not for Threes (1997):** first heavy sampling + vocals; **baroque/stately, modal-minor**
  color; "buoyant, warm" vs the colder Black Dog sound; high melody over warm pad bed. Björk
  "Lilith" = jazzy open voicings + acoustic color. [MED]
- **Rest Proof Clockwork (1999):** **FS1R** central (FM + formant). "Ralome" = **melody in
  octaves**, spread/consonant. **Gentle Giant** cited → complex multipart voice-leading. Bright,
  major-leaning, bittersweet; "machines do the singing." [HIGH gear / MED voicing]
- **Double Figure (2001):** darker, rhythm-forward but still melody-driven; dense interlocking
  synth lines ("Squance"); bittersweet = bright brass-ish synths in a minor context. Keys
  documented for a couple tracks (Hooktheory). [MED]
- **Spokes (2003):** tuned percussion (bells/glock/xylophone) carries harmony; "narrative
  complexity to chord and melody patterns"; more accessible. [MED]
- **Tekkonkinkreet OST (2006):** explicit brief to move nostalgic-acoustic → dissonant-synthetic
  → hybrid resolution; vibraphone/strings/gamelan against breakbeats. Open orchestral voicing. [MED]
- **Scintilli (2011):** **sine-wave synthetic vocals** (pure additive harmonic control);
  physical modeling; angular, geometric melodies; "gorgeous chord sequences." [HIGH for sine
  vocals / MED voicing]
- **Reachy Prints (2014):** trademark **shimmering/luminous detuned wavetable pads**; lush,
  openly consonant; 70s prog/folk feel; molasses bass under bright top lines. [MED]
- **Polymer (2019) — best-documented:** **comb-filtered percussive FM → "euphoric melodies";**
  organic↔synthetic timbre morphing; **piano-led composition**; open register spacing (bass
  anchor / mid harmonic cloud / sparse top). Tools: Falcon, Loom, Icarus, ABL3, Madrona. [HIGH]
- **Feorm Falorx (2022):** **"chamber-techno"** — open spread voicings across octaves; bass
  rumble → mid synth harmony → shrill high harmonics; **Newfangled Generate** pendulum
  oscillators give organic detune without a chorus plugin; "unusual chord progressions." [MED-HIGH]

## Cross-cutting voicing principles (the payoff)

1. **Melody-first, played, human.** Piano-composed, then voiced on synths. Not generative.
2. **Open / spread voicings across registers** — bass anchor low, harmonic pad in the mids,
   sparse melodic top: a **chamber-ensemble** layout even in all-synth tracks.
3. **Octave doubling & parallel motion** for fullness (documented: "Ralome").
4. **Bittersweet = modal mixture** — major-leaning melody over minor/modal harmony.
5. **Triadic core + 6ths/sus for warmth**, occasional upper-register color; not dense jazz
   extensions.
6. **FM for the bright/bell/piano/"euphoric" leads** — DX7 → FS1R → comb-filtered FM.
7. **Warmth/shimmer from detuned wavetable unison + physical modeling** (subtle, organic; not a
   heavy chorus). Pendulum/Lissajous oscillators (Generate) for organic drift.
8. **Tuned percussion as a harmonic voice** (bells/glockenspiel/xylophone).
9. **Consonant, diatonic/modal, 12-TET.** No microtuning.

## Implications for GLITCHFIELD (spec-only, not built)

This is the artist study that **validates** the voicing revamp already built:

- **The COLD-band triads are appropriate here** (unlike for Autechre). Plaid voice real chords —
  but **open/spread**, which matches the fifth/octave dyads and the open triad (`triadOpen`)
  choices better than close triads.
- **Register lift is right** — Plaid put the melodic/bell material up top over a low bass anchor;
  GLITCHFIELD's octShift does exactly that.
- **Octave doubling** (the `oct` recipe) is a documented Plaid move.
- **Lean on the `fm` machine** for the bright/bell/euphoric leads; **superwave detune** for the
  shimmer/warmth.
- **Bittersweet modal mixture:** the existing key/scale + harmony traversals can put a
  major-leaning line over a minor context — very Plaid. Worth a preset that does this.
- Missing/nice-to-have: a **tuned-percussion** timbre as a harmonic voice; **comb-filtered FM**
  flavor; and Plaid's **piano-first, "find the next chord"** ethos is compositional, not an
  engine feature (GLITCHFIELD's authored degree sequences already embody it).

## Contrast with the Autechre notes

- **Autechre:** generative counterpoint, scale-forced Markov note choice, deliberate
  detuning/inharmonicity, anti-12-TET, reverb-as-harmony. Chords are *emergent*, not stacked.
- **Plaid:** hand-played consonant chords, open chamber voicings, FM bells, subtle warmth
  detuning, strictly tonal/12-TET.

For "melodic glitch," **Plaid is the closer target** — favor open chord voicings, high melodic
register, FM bell leads, and bittersweet modal color over Autechre's generative abstraction.

## Caveats

Gear and composition-philosophy are well-sourced; specific chord voicings, progressions, and
scales are almost never documented (Plaid avoid theory talk), so MED/LOW voicing items are
aesthetic inference, not transcription. Mbuki Mvuki (1991) is essentially undocumented.
