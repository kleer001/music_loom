### Sequencing Facts

**Album Gaps Between Tracks**
- Red Book CD standard: 2-second pre-gap (150 frames at Index 0) is mandatory before Track 1 — https://support.discmakers.com/hc/en-us/articles/209223248
- Mastering engineers add 100-300 ms gap after track index point to prevent upcut by CD player unmute circuits (which vary 0-200 ms) — https://csr.hdsupply.com/best-pause-time-between-tracks-on-a-cd/
- Between-track gaps not standardized by CD manufacturers; one approach is 1-2 second gap between index flag and audio — https://support.discmakers.com/hc/en-us/articles/209223248

**Platform Definitions: Single, EP, Album**

Spotify (streaming service):
- Single: 1-3 tracks, under 30 minutes
- EP: 4-6 tracks, under 30 minutes
- Album: 7+ tracks or over 30 minutes
— https://support.cdbaby.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008275672

Apple Music (streaming service):
- Single: 1-3 tracks, all under 10 minutes, total under 30 minutes
- EP: (1-3 tracks with at least one 10+ minutes) OR (4-6 tracks, under 30 minutes total)
- Album: 7+ tracks or over 30 minutes
— https://help.awal.com/hc/en-us/articles/7882058990867

Grammy Awards / Recording Academy (awards body):
- EP: 3-5 tracks (defined as "between a single and a full CD")
- Album: minimum 5 tracks AND 15 minutes total runtime, OR any length with 30+ minutes total runtime
— https://grammy.com/news/whats-difference-grammy-album-vs-record-year-explained, https://jamaica-gleaner.com/article/entertainment/20191121/koffee-cops-grammy-nomination-ep-academy-defines-5-track-rapture

Official Charts Company / UK Charts (chart authority):
- Single: max 4 tracks, max 25 minutes (single bundle) OR one digital track under 15 minutes
- EP/Album boundary: more than 4 tracks OR over 25 minutes duration = album
— https://newindustryfocus.com/tags/occ-chart-eligibility, https://mail.45worlds.com/topic/102293

**Album Sequencing in Streaming Era**
- Opening track is crucial for "drawing listeners in" and establishing tone despite skip availability — https://djbooth.net/features/2023-02-13-album-sequencing-in-streaming-era/
- Closing track "often leaves the strongest impression" and deserves equal care — https://djbooth.net/features/2023-02-13-album-sequencing-in-streaming-era/
- Common sequencing errors: lacking logical progression, overusing interludes, ending weakly, poor dynamic balance, too much consecutive slow/fast songs, treating album as isolated tracks — https://djbooth.net/features/2023-02-13-album-sequencing-in-streaming-era/
- Modern artists either optimize for playlist placement or use creative interludes to maintain intended flow — https://djbooth.net/features/2023-02-13-album-sequencing-in-streaming-era/

**Album Sequencing Principles**
- Purpose: create narrative or flow guiding listener from beginning to climax to conclusion — https://djbooth.net/features/2023-02-13-album-sequencing-in-streaming-era/
- Common principles: narrative progression, contrast (dense followed by sparse), momentum (up-tempo to sustain energy), thematic grouping — https://djbooth.net/features/2023-02-13-album-sequencing-in-streaming-era/

**Indie/Alternative Rock Context**
- Indie rock appropriates punk's DIY aesthetic: resistance to commercialism, glorification of amateurism, vintage instruments/equipment — https://drum.lib.umd.edu/bitstream/handle/1903/17444/Essential%20Indie.pdf
- Common characteristics: balance of pop accessibility with noise, playfulness with pop formulae, sensitive lyrics masked by tonal abrasiveness and irony, concern with "authenticity," cultivation of "regular guy/girl" image — https://drum.lib.umd.edu/bitstream/handle/1903/17444/Essential%20Indie.pdf
- Indie-folk emerged early 21st century when indie rock songwriters adopted folk music cues; by 2000s encompassed neo-Celtic folk (Decemberists) to Appalachian harmonies (Fleet Foxes) — https://drum.lib.umd.edu/bitstream/handle/1903/17444/Essential%20Indie.pdf

**Alt-Country Fusion Context**
- Alternative country incorporates influences from: alternative rock, indie rock, punk rock, heartland rock, Southern rock, progressive country, outlaw country, neotraditional country, Texas country, Red Dirt, roots rock, indie folk, folk rock, rockabilly, bluegrass, honky tonk — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_country
