# Per-Genre Song Norms and Lyric Craft (Non-EDM)

## Findings

### General Song Structures and Forms

- Verse/Chorus and Verse/Pre-Chorus/Chorus are the most common forms across genres — [https://online.berklee.edu/courses/songwriting-writing-hit-songs](https://online.berklee.edu/courses/songwriting-writing-hit-songs)
- AABA song structure (verse/refrain) is an alternative traditional form — [https://online.berklee.edu/courses/songwriting-writing-hit-songs](https://online.berklee.edu/courses/songwriting-writing-hit-songs)
- Bridges are used as Primary Bridges or Pre-Choruses (Transitional Bridges) — [https://online.berklee.edu/courses/lyric-writing-writing-lyrics-to-music](https://online.berklee.edu/courses/lyric-writing-writing-lyrics-to-music)
- Section lengths typically range from 8 to 24 bars across genres — [https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure](https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure)

### Hip-Hop/Rap Specifics

- Standard rap verse is 16 bars; verses can also be 8, 24, or 32 bars depending on structure and tempo — [https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure](https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure)
- **Boom Bap**: 85-95 BPM; typical structure is Intro (4 bars) - Verse (16 bars) - Chorus (8 bars) - Verse (16 bars) - Chorus (8 bars) - Outro — [https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure](https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure)
- **Trap**: 130-150 BPM; structure is Intro (8 bars) - Verse (16 bars) - Chorus (8 bars) - Verse (16 bars) - Chorus (8 bars) - Outro — [https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure](https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure)
- **Drill**: 140-145 BPM; typically Intro (8 bars) - Chorus (8 bars) - Verse (16 bars) - Chorus (8 bars) - Verse (16 bars) - Chorus (8 bars) - Outro — [https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure](https://emastered.com/blog/rap-song-structure)

### Blues

- 12-bar Blues is the most common form using an AAB pattern (most frequent) — [https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/music/music-theory/music-theory-popular-genres-and-forms-265008/](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/music/music-theory/music-theory-popular-genres-and-forms-265008/)
- Also exists in 8-bar, 16-bar, and 24-bar variations — [https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/music/music-theory/music-theory-popular-genres-and-forms-265008/](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/music/music-theory/music-theory-popular-genres-and-forms-265008/)
- BPM range: 70-120 BPM with shuffle feel — [https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/music/music-theory/music-theory-popular-genres-and-forms-265008/](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/music/music-theory/music-theory-popular-genres-and-forms-265008/)

### Lyric Writing Techniques from Pat Pattison

- **Object Writing**: A sensory technique to engage all five senses in writing exercises — [Search result summary on Pat Pattison's "Writing Better Lyrics"](https://a-lyric.com/)
- Use imagery and sensory details to paint a picture; illustrate feelings through actions or settings rather than stating them — [Search result summary on Pat Pattison's "Writing Better Lyrics"](https://a-lyric.com/)
- Emphasize variety and subtlety in rhyming; use different rhyme schemes and incorporate near rhymes to add complexity — [Search result summary on Pat Pattison's "Writing Better Lyrics"](https://a-lyric.com/)
- Different song structures serve different lyrical purposes (verse-chorus, AABA, free verse); stick to a consistent form to aid flow and coherence — [Search result summary on Pat Pattison's "Writing Better Lyrics"](https://a-lyric.com/)

### Hook and Chorus Craft

- The hook is the most memorable part of the song, often the chorus or a catchy phrase — [https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947](https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947)
- Create an effective hook by: focusing on a simple, catchy melody that's easy to sing along with; use repetition to reinforce the hook; ensure the hook encapsulates the essence or emotion of the song — [https://fastrhymes.com/blog/how-to-write-a-chorus-that-sticks-tips-for-crafting-memorable-hooks](https://fastrhymes.com/blog/how-to-write-a-chorus-that-sticks-tips-for-crafting-memorable-hooks)
- The chorus should be the emotional and melodic peak of the song, conveying the main message or theme concisely and memorably — [https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947](https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947)
- Best choruses distill the entire song's message into a few powerful lines; avoid cramming too many ideas into your chorus — [https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947](https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947)
- The chorus should stand out from verses in melody, lyrics, and intensity; use a different chord progression or key for the chorus compared to verses — [https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947](https://wisseloord.org/?p=33947)
