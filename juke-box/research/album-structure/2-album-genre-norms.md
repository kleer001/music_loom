# Album Norms by Genre — The Numbers

## Findings

### Song Length Trends (1995-2024)
- Hip-hop: 3.2 minutes average by 2022, down 30% from 4.5 minutes in 1995 — https://hmc.chartmetric.com/taylor-swift-eras-tour-shorter-songs-bigger-albums/
- Pop: 3.36 minutes average by 2022, down 18% from 4 minutes — https://hmc.chartmetric.com/taylor-swift-eras-tour-shorter-songs-bigger-albums/
- Country: 3.35 minutes (2022), down from 3.48 minutes in 2019 — https://hmc.chartmetric.com/taylor-swift-eras-tour-shorter-songs-bigger-albums/

### Album Track Count & Length (Streaming Era)
- Pop albums: 10-15 tracks, 35-45 minutes typical — https://blog.groover.co/en/tips/how-long-is-an-album-en/
- Hip-hop albums: increased 16% to over 15 tracks per album by 2022; historically 10-12 tracks but now often exceed 60 minutes — https://hmc.chartmetric.com/taylor-swift-eras-tour-shorter-songs-bigger-albums/ and https://blog.groover.co/en/tips/how-long-is-an-album-en/
- Optimal album length across genres: 30-45 minutes with ~11 tracks for higher completion rates — https://blog.groover.co/en/tips/how-long-is-an-album-en/
- Album track count increased around 2019 coinciding with song length decline — https://hmc.chartmetric.com/taylor-swift-eras-tour-shorter-songs-bigger-albums/

### Streaming Mechanics
- On Spotify, any track played >30 seconds counts as a stream, creating incentives for quantity over duration — https://hmc.chartmetric.com/taylor-swift-eras-tour-shorter-songs-bigger-albums/

### Interludes, Skits, Intros, Outros
- Hip-hop: skits were standard in classic albums (post-1988) serving as continuity devices and palette cleansers, but are now declining due to streaming preferences (playlists vs. full-album listening) — https://popmatters.com/how-to-begin-end-album and https://www.complex.com/music/a/gabriel-alvarez/the-50-greatest-hip-hop-skits
- Intros/interludes/outros in hip-hop were meant to show album intent and continuity — https://popmatters.com/how-to-begin-end-album
- No specific data found on typical count of interludes/skits per album by genre

### EP & Mini-Album Norms
- General EP standard: 3-7 tracks, 10-30 minutes (contemporary: 4-9 tracks, 15-30 minutes) — https://www.usemogul.com/post/what-is-an-ep-in-music-a-comprehensive-guide
- K-pop mini-albums: 5-6 tracks increasingly common (resurgence from older 4-track or 7-track patterns) — https://seoulbeats.com/?p=249873
