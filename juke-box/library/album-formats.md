# Album formats and lengths

How many tracks, how long, by format and by genre. Each number carries its source and the weight of that source.

## Single, EP or album

The definitions differ. Choose the target first.

| Authority | Single | EP | Album | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spotify | 1–3 tracks, under 30 min | 4–6 tracks, under 30 min | 7+ tracks or over 30 min | CD Baby help, https://support.cdbaby.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008275672 |
| Apple Music | 1–3 tracks, each under 10 min, under 30 min total | 4–6 tracks under 30 min, or 1–3 tracks with one over 10 min | 7+ tracks or over 30 min | AWAL help, https://help.awal.com/hc/en-us/articles/7882058990867 |
| Recording Academy (Grammys) | — | 3–5 tracks | 5+ tracks and 15+ min, or 30+ min at any track count | grammy.com |
| Official Charts Company (UK) | up to 4 tracks, up to 25 min | — | more than 4 tracks or over 25 min | trade site and a forum; weak |

A set of 7 or more tracks, or more than 30 minutes, is an album on both streaming services.

## Song length

| Measure | Value | Source and weight |
|---|---|---|
| Hip-hop song, mean | 3.2 min in 2022, down 30% from 4.5 min in 1995 | Chartmetric data analysis |
| Pop song, mean | 3.36 min in 2022, down 18% from 4 min | Chartmetric data analysis |
| Country song, mean | 3.35 min in 2022, from 3.48 min in 2019 | Chartmetric data analysis |
| Hot 100, median | 4:10 in 2000, 3:30 in 2018, 3:07 in 2021, about 3:00 in 2024 | WeRaveYou, 2026 (blog that cites research) |
| Pop, form | First chorus within 30 s, often 15 s; intro 0–5 s; a bridge in 15–25% of songs | WeRaveYou, 2026 (blog, single source) |

Chartmetric: https://hmc.chartmetric.com/taylor-swift-eras-tour-shorter-songs-bigger-albums/

The streaming pressure toward short songs comes from payment per stream (a stream counts after 30 s). A local album does not have that pressure. Choose song length for the music.

## Album size by genre

| Genre or format | Norm | Source and weight |
|---|---|---|
| Hip-hop album | More than 15 tracks by 2022, up 16%; 10–12 tracks before streaming | Chartmetric data analysis, plus a blog |
| Pop album | 10–15 tracks, 35–45 min | Groover (blog) |
| Any genre | 30–45 min and about 11 tracks give higher completion | Groover (blog only) |
| K-pop mini-album | 5–7 tracks, one title song, often an instrumental or spoken intro or outro | Fan blogs |
| K-pop full album | 8+ tracks | Fan blogs |
| Lo-fi or chillhop | 1–3 min tracks, made to loop | Production blogs |
| Ambient | Continuous or looping pieces; Eno's *Music for Airports* has four long pieces from tape loops | Single source |

No sourced numbers were found for rock, indie, metal, punk, folk, country, jazz, electronic or soundtrack albums. The studied default of 10–12 tracks and 35–45 minutes is a safe start for those.

## Sizes for a juke-box album

MiniMax Music 3 renders one song at a time, up to 360 s. On an RTX 3090 a 3-minute song takes about 4.5 minutes to render, so each take costs more time than the song lasts.

| Format | Tracks | Run time | Render time for 2 takes per track, RTX 3090 |
|---|---|---|---|
| EP | 4–6 | 15–25 min | about 35–55 min |
| Short album | 8–10 | 28–38 min | about 1.2–1.5 h |
| Full album | 11–14 | 38–50 min | about 1.7–2.1 h |

Render times assume 3-minute songs at the measured 272 s for one 180 s request (4.5 min per take). They are estimates from one measurement.
