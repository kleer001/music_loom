## Attribution

`machine/LICENSE.txt` travels with the machine loops and names the Sonic Pi release. VCSL is credited in the rows above and in `README.md`. Greensleeves is the one asset here whose licence asks for something: attribution to the Mutopia Project, plus share-alike, if a derived arrangement is distributed.

## Related sources not held here

Recorded for the trail, since instruments built here draw on them and the derived files live in cyber_synth rather than the pantry:

- **Vital presets** → `cyber_synth/data/patches/` (83 derived patch JSONs). Source: [instatetragrammaton/Patches](https://github.com/instatetragrammaton/Patches), "Matt Tytel Vital" folder — **CC-BY-4.0, attribution owed**. A CC0 alternative is [atsushieno/open-vital-resources](https://github.com/atsushieno/open-vital-resources). Raw `.vital` files are not vendored anywhere.
- **Wavetable banks** → `cyber_synth/data/wavetables/` (8 derived coefficient JSONs). Source: [smpldsnds/wavedit-online](https://github.com/smpldsnds/wavedit-online), `/samples` — CC0-1.0. Banks imported: ZAP, VOXSYNTH, VIRUS_SA, WAVETABL.
- **Soundfonts** → ferine_town `web/samples/` (untracked, regenerated by its own script). FluidR3_GM is MIT (Frank Wen); MusyngKite is CC-BY-SA 3.0.
