## How you will know it worked

The measurable claim. Not "sounds right" — something the render harness can read. Spectral centre in a band, a modulation depth at a period, a decay time, a level relationship between two layers, a pitch within cents of a target.

Written before building, it is a prediction. Written after, it is a description of whatever happened.
